    QUICK RULES
    Quick tracking: Record net damage after Armour/AP using − buttons: shields are depleted before HP. Use + to restore HP, or ◈ controls to adjust shields.
    Generators: 30 HP, 2 Armour each; occupy their entire cube. If the weapon has ESD (n), deplete n shields on a successful hit first. Then resolve hits against remaining shields, Armour reduced by AP, and HP.
    Deploy Shields: A Red unit in an adjacent cube spends a Long Auxiliary action; the generator gains Energy Shield (3), once per generator per game.
    UNSC Defence Shields: While a unit is in a cube containing a UNSC Defence Shield, that unit has +1 Armour against Shoot actions.
    On destruction: Remove the generator; every adjacent cube suffers Frag (5), Blast. Resolve this explosion on the tabletop.
    Duration: Up to 8 rounds, or until either team reaches 30 VP.

    The optional regeneration switch is OFF by default because this mission page does not explicitly say whether generator shields regenerate. The core Energy Shield (n) rule grants regeneration to models. Quick − buttons apply already-calculated damage to shields first, then HP (they do not calculate Armour or AP). Quick + buttons restore HP only. Use the separate shield controls to correct shield values. The detailed attack resolver remains available for AP/ESD attacks. Progress is saved automatically in this browser.
